What it does

Everything important in one window.

Not a web wrapper. Not a phone mirror. A native desktop app with the protocol stack underneath.

01

Live video

Receives the Mini 1 H.265/HEVC live-view stream and decodes it into the desktop application.

02

Telemetry

Flight state, altitude, speed, battery, attitude, home state and diagnostic information.

03

Flight control

Takeoff, land, RTH, arm/disarm and continuous virtual-stick control with flight modes.

04

Camera & gimbal

Mouse gimbal control, photo, recording, zoom and supported exposure controls.

05

Protocol console

Inspect and send supported DUML commands from the native in-flight console.

06

Pi discovery

The PC app discovers the Raspberry Pi bridge and handles the connection path.
