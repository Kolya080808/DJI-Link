WHERE HELP IS NEEDED MOST

Not every contribution needs to be code.

Several of the project's biggest bottlenecks are research, hardware access, and keeping a growing body of documentation coherent.

01 · RESEARCH

Media protocol — highest priority

The media side is currently one of the biggest unknowns in DJI Link. Unlike flight control and telemetry, there is very little reliable public research to build on. Packet captures, DJI Fly observations, protocol analysis, comparisons with other DJI aircraft, and reproducible experiments are extremely valuable.

Research is more useful than implementation here. Once the protocol is understood, the implementation can be handled in DJI Link.

Share media research ↗
02 · HARDWARE

Xbox controller testing

PS-controller testing is available locally, but I do not currently have an Xbox controller. Testing buttons, axes, dead zones, mappings, and platform-specific behavior on Xbox hardware would directly help the controller support planned for 2.0.0.

Share controller results ↗
03 · DOCUMENTATION

Help keep the knowledge usable

The Wiki and reverse-engineering corpus are growing quickly. Finding outdated statements, contradictions, broken links, missing cross-references, unclear research notes, or duplicated information is a real contribution.

You do not need to write code to improve this project.

Report a documentation issue ↗
04 · FUTURE

AI research & new ideas

AI is a longer-term direction for 3.0.0. Research, references, experiments, and ideas are welcome: from bringing useful capabilities found on newer DJI aircraft to the Mini, to exploring capabilities that were never implemented for it in the first place.

Have a useful, unusual, or technically interesting idea? Open an issue or start a Discussion.

Before large changes, open an issue so we can agree on the approach. For research, include what you observed, what you inferred, and whether it was reproduced on real hardware.

Protect the aircraft.

Test with propellers removed where possible, use only hardware you own or are authorized to operate, and never trade flight safety for a faster result.
