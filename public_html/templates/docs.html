Flight control

DJI Link includes takeoff, landing, RTH, arm/disarm and virtual-stick control. Flight control is advanced functionality and should be tested with the aircraft secured.

07 · SAFETY

Before you fly

DJI Link is unofficial software that can control a real aircraft. Use only on owned/authorized hardware, keep clear propellers while validating configuration, maintain line of sight and follow local aviation requirements.
