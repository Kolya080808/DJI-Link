# Code Of Conduct

We want DJI Link to be a focused, respectful place for engineering and research.

Participants must:

- be respectful and specific when discussing code, hardware, and research;
- welcome questions from people with different levels of experience;
- give credit for prior work and distinguish measurements from assumptions;
- keep safety, privacy, and legal constraints in mind when sharing experiments.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberately unsafe instructions are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive and safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact listed in the project profile.
